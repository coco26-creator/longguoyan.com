About This Expression

The Cellar Reserved Emperor's Supreme 30 selects premium Hongyingzi glutinous sorghum from Maotai Town. Following the traditional Daqu Kunsha craftsmanship, it undergoes nine rounds of steaming, eight rounds of fermentation, and seven rounds of wine extraction to produce the base liquor. After being sealed in ceramic vats and aged for years, it matures naturally with time.

The bottle is adorned with dragon-scale relief carvings, while the outer gift box features leather and gold-stamping craftsmanship, reflecting exquisite details and a dignified, noble style. Jiu Wu Zhi Zun symbolises supremacy and perfection — not only a masterpiece of Sauce Aroma liquor craftsmanship, but a precious vintage refined by time, ideal for collection and connoisseurship.

Tasting Profile

Appearance Full-bodied with a rich, intense sauce aroma. Pale yellow and clear.
Nose Rich and intense sauce aroma with elegant aged character — decades of cellar ageing reveal a bouquet of extraordinary complexity
Palate Smooth and delicate on the palate. The middle section is full and smooth with a harmonious, balanced combination of layered flavours. No harsh edges.

The Craft Behind the Spirit

01
Selection of Raw Materials

Only premium Hongyingzi glutinous red sorghum grown in the highlands of Guizhou, combined with natural Guizhou water and wheat, forms the foundation of every batch.

02
Traditional Daqu Kunsha Process

Following the traditional Daqu Kunsha craftsmanship — high-temperature starter preparation, high-temperature stacking fermentation, and high-temperature distillation. The defining process of authentic Sauce Aroma Distillery.

03
Nine Rounds of Steaming

The mash undergoes nine separate rounds of steaming, eight rounds of fermentation, and seven rounds of wine extraction across a full annual production cycle.

04
30-Year Ceramic Vat Ageing

The finest base spirits are sealed in ceramic vats and aged for 30 years, maturing naturally with time. The unique microclimate of Maotai Town transforms raw spirit into a transcendent expression.

05
Master Blending

National-level distilling masters carefully select and blend aged spirits to achieve the precise Cellar Supreme 30 profile — rich, balanced, and unforgettable.
