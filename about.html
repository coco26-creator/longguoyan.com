COMPANY PROFILE

Guizhou Longguoyan Distillery Co., Ltd. is located in Maotai Town, Guizhou Province, the core production area of premium sauce-aroma liquor.

With years of brewing heritage, the company integrates production, culture and brand development to deliver high-quality aged liquor to domestic and overseas markets.
