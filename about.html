COMPANY PROFILE

Guizhou Longguoyan Distillery Co., Ltd. is located in Maotai Town, Guizhou Province, China — the world-renowned core production area for sauce-aroma liquor. As a long-established and reputable liquor enterprise in Maotai Town, Longguoyan has always adhered to the inheritance and development of ancient brewing craftsmanship, and has been recognized as a Guizhou Time-Honored Brand by the Department of Commerce of Guizhou Province.

Longguoyan liquor selects high-quality local Hongyingzi glutinous sorghum, wheat and water from Guizhou as its raw materials. We uphold the concept of natural, pure-grain solid-state fermentation, and follow the scientific and unique traditional sauce-aroma brewing craftsmanship. Each product is elaborately brewed by national-level distilling masters.

Guizhou Longguoyan Distillery Co., Ltd. always adheres to the development strategy of Quality First and Long-Termism, strictly controls every link in the production process, and ensures the steady and long-term development of the enterprise.

In terms of brand building, Longguoyan launched its global cultural brand strategy in 2019, and has continuously carried out high-standard brand promotion projects, including CCTV advertising, national high-speed rail advertising, highway outdoor advertising, and high-end tasting events.
